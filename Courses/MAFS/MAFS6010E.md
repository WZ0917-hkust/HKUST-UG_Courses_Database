# Description
Entrepreneurship and Venture CapitalBuilding Successful Startups 
This course aims to provide students with a solid understanding of the entrepreneurial process and venture capital financing. It covers all aspects of building entrepreneurial startups, including identifying and shaping opportunities, creating a business model and plan, raising funds, managing a startup, and exiting. Additionally, we will explore venture capital basics and how to work with venture capitalists, as it is a key source of funding for most entrepreneurial startups.

# Description
Chemistry of Energy 
This course will cover topics in chemistry related to energy production, storage and transport. This will include theoretical, including quantum mechanical and thermodynamical, descriptions of energy processes for chemical energy storage, light harvesting, charge storage, energy and charge transfer, heat storage and transfer, and other material processes, while touching upon selected practical aspects of energy production and storage, such as material synthesis, device fabrication, and the relative economics of energy technologies and energy policy.

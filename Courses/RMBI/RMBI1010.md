# Description
Risk Management in Financial Institutions 
Risk exists in every aspect of our daily life, and risk management is essential for any organization to achieve their business objectives. This course provides an opportunity for students to examine the various kinds of risk existing in society, and learn about the benefits and impacts of risk management. The course focuses on risk measurement and analysis in enterprise business and modern financial institutions. Learning activities consist of a mixture of lectures, case studies and lab exercises.

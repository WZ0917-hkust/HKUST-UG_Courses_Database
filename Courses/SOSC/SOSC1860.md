# Description
Population and Society 
This blended learning course introduces the main measures and concepts in the field of international population studies, contemporary issues concerning the interrelationships between population and social and economic development, and examples of applications of a knowledge of population to wider social concerns, and shows how they relate to issues of concern in research, planning and policy development.

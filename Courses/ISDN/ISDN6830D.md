# Description
Artificial Intelligence + Internet of Things for Healthcare 
This course explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) in smart health context. Students will learn how intelligent algorithms and connected devices work together to enhance patient care, streamline medical processes, and improve diagnostic accuracy. The curriculum covers foundational AI methods, IoT architecture, real-time health monitoring, wellness management applications, and essential knowledge of data privacy, ethical considerations, and regulatory compliance in AIoT. Through lectures, practical labs, and a team-based final project, students will gain hands-on experience and develop the skills needed to harness AIoT technologies for proactive, personalized health solutions.

# Description
Advanced Engineering Research Competencies 
This course equips students with the competencies essential to engineering research study, including but not limited to data analysis and interpretation, academic writing, and research ethics and integrity. It also equips students with the knowledge of the nature of research education and how to cope with common challenges for research students. It prepares junior students for the research journey and strengthens senior students' research skills as well as professional skills such as teamwork and communication.

# Description
Robotics Special Project: Intelligent Racing Competition 2025 
The NXP Cup National University Students Intelligent Car Racing is a national competition for young engineers. Student teams will design and manufacture the racing model car for tackling different missions and challenges which are provided by the organizer. Normally, a project report is required. Enrollment in the project course may require instructor's approval. May be graded PP.

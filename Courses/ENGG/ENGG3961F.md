# Description
Robotics Special Project: ABU ROBOCON 2025 
The Asia-Pacific Robot Contest (ABU Robocon) is an Asian Oceanian College robot competition for young engineers. Student teams will design and manufacture robots for tackling different missions and challenges which are provided by the organizer. Normally, a project report is required. Enrollment in the project course may require instructor's approval. May be graded PP.

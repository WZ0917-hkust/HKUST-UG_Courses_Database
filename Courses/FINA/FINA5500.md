# Description
VC Entrepreneurships 
This course will cover how the Venture Capital (VC) industry works, all the parties involved, and the dynamics therein. By illustrating real world examples in some of the largest and most respected and successful VC firms in the world, students will learn how VCs raise money, how they construct a fund portfolio, how they make investment decisions, how VC returns are calculated, and how to land a job in this mysterious sector of high finance.

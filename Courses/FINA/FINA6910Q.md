# Description
Overview of Alternative Investments
The Overview of Alternative Investments course aims to provide students with a holistic introduction to the alternative investment ecosystem, covering sectors such as real estate, infrastructure, credit, hedge funds, secondaries, and others. Please note that the private equity and venture capital sectors will not be covered in this course in detail as they will be covered in other dedicated units offered by the MBA programme.

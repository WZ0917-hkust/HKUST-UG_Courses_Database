# Description
Responsible Finance 
This course is designed to give students a thorough overview of responsible finance and ESG. The first part covers fundamental topics related to ESG. The second part focuses more on project and firm valuation with an ESG perspective. It also covers financial models that will allow one to understand how to incorporate ESG factors in financial decisions.

# Description
Fundamentals of Business Finance 
For BIBU and non-SB&M students. This course provides an introduction to corporate finance for students who may not have background in accounting and business statistics. Topics include financial statement analysis, time value of money, bond and stock valuation, capital budgeting, risk and return concepts and cost of capital.

# Description
Descriptions of Language for Chinese Teachers 
The course provides students with a broad grounding on the nature and structure of language by introducing them to linguistic analysis, with emphasis on that of Chinese. Topics to be discussed include the subfields of linguistics such as phonetics, phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, and pragmatics. For MA(ILE) students or students with program director or course instructor's approval only.

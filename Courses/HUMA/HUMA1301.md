# Description
World Literature and Film 
This course explores the complex interplay between literature and cinema. Selected novels, short stories and plays are analyzed in relation to their cinematic adaptations to gain an understanding of the masterpieces of world literature, and the possibilities and challenges involved in their cinematic manifestations.

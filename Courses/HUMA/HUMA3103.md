# Description
Making Chamber Music B 
Through weekly coaching sessions and public performances, students will learn all facets of chamber music making at a high level and expand their repertoire on their instrument. Students will also learn team-building skills and develop attentiveness to partners in an ensemble. This is a course for students who are highly motivated, and sufficiently trained and experienced in classical music performance. Enrollment in the course requires instructor's approval based on audition outcome.

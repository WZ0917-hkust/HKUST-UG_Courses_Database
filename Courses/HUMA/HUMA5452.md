# Description
Politics of Fan Culture Studies 
This course explores the theories, debates, and challenges surrounding the study of media theories of fan culture as a site of political engagement. Its central problematic is the question of how new media forms and technologies engender practices of spectatorship and consumption and how these practices mediate new forms of political activities. Topics of discussion will include spectatorial practice between cinema and animation, media convergence and participatory culture, intersections of gender/sexuality and fan practice, fan work as immaterial labor, politics of world-making, etc.

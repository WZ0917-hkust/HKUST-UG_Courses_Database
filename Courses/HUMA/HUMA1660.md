# Description
Introduction to Chinese Art 
This course is a chronological and thematic survey of Chinese visual art from the Neolithic period to modern times. Topics introduced include ancient Chinese bronze and jade, pottery and ceramics, Buddhist art and sculpture, calligraphy and seal carving, garden and architecture.

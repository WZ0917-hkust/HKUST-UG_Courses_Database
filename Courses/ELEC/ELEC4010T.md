# Description
Business for Electronic Engineers 
This course provides electronic engineering and IT students a comprehensive introduction to the essential knowledge involved in the conduction of business. Topics covered include: the organization of technology enterprises, fundamentals of financial and managerial accounting, engineering economics, supply chain and quality, marketing, managerial finance and introduction to mathematical finance, and technology and entrepreneurship.

# Description
Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering Colloquia
The Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering Colloquia invites distinguished researchers from chemical and biomolecular engineering to present their groundbreaking work, offering CBE faculty, staff, and students a unique opportunity to delve into the latest advancements in the field. The primary objective is to foster an environment conducive to intellectual discourse and idea exchange, encouraging participants to engage in thoughtful discussions and debates. Throughout the course, students will have direct access to these speakers, enabling them to gain a deeper appreciation for the complexities and nuances of the field. The curriculum encompasses a diverse array of topics, spanning chemical engineering, biotechnology, materials science, synthetic biology, and so on. Grade P or F.

# Description
Advanced Membrane Technology for Water and Energy 
This course explores the intricate relationship between water and energy systems and examines advanced membrane technologies that address challenges and opportunities at the nexus of these vital resources. Students will investigate innovative solutions and interdisciplinary approaches to enhance sustainability and efficiency in water and energy nexus and its management.

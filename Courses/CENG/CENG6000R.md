# Description
Introduction to Molecular Dynamics Simulations 
This course aims to deliver both the fundamental understanding and hands-on practicing of molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, which play significant roles in many science and engineering fields today. Students will learn the fundamentals underlying MD simulations and the skills to design, run, visualize and analyze MD simulations. The course will also feature hands-on MD projects related to problems in chemical and biomolecular engineering.

# Description
Honors Research 
The honors research teaches the student to utilize relevant theories and methodologies in a concrete research task. It requires the student to conduct more in-depth research based on the capstone project, to collect and analyze relevant material and to complete a thesis that meets academic standards of using citations and references, statement of a problem or an issue, and a clearly identified theoretical point. Enrollment in the course requires approval of the course coordinator.

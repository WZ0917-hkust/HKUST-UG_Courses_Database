# Description
Environmental, Social, and Governance Fundamentals 
This course examines the importance of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors in managing modern corporations. Throughout the course, students will examine real-life examples to highlight why and to which extent ESG matters for firms and investors operating in various contexts.

# Description
Leadership and Decision Making
This course will equip managers with the skills and knowledge needed to make high-quality decisions, distinguishing themselves and their organizations. By providing practical tools and frameworks, participants will enhance their decision-making abilities and drive long-term success. Emphasizing that quality management decisions can be acquired and practiced, this course focuses on the profound impact of leaders' choices and their role in organizational achievements.

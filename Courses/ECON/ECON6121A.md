# Description
Topics in Economic dynamics I 
The 7-week macroeconomics course covering solution methods in business cycle and DSGE models, bounded rationality - adaptive learning vs. rational expectations, equilibrium determinacy and expectational stability applied to monetary policy rules, and introductions to both financial frictions macro and tractable HANK (Heterogenous Agent New Keynesian) models.

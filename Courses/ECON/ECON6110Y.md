# Description
Health Economics 
This course applies the tools of economic analysis to issues about the organization, delivery, and financing of health care.  Topics be covered include (but are not restricted to) demand for health, health services, and health insurance; supply of health care services, such as physicians, hospitals, drugs, medical technology, and of health insurance; health-care policies, systems, and reforms. As time permits, a few more topics will be covered, including production, demand, supply, resource allocation, and markets for health and health care, with special reference to the risks and risk-bearing associated with sickness, information imperfection and asymmetry, and insurance and agency problems; institutional structure of the health and health-care markets, especially with regard to the characteristics of for-profit and non-profit organizations, as well as health-care institutions and systems in different countries; and formulation and implementation of policies and regulations in health and health care, plus efficiency, equity, and need basis of government intervention in market activities and initiatives towards reform.

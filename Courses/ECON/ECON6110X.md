# Description
Econometrics in Business: Strategy and Decision Making 
The course seeks to meet the business practitioners' needs for applied research and analysis in the business world, with the primary focus on strategy and decision making. This course develops and reinforces the fundamental tools of descriptive statistics, estimation techniques, statistical hypotheses, multivariate regression and statistical inference in the context of business strategy and decision making. Particular attention is applied to the estimation of cross-section and time series data in business, though we will touch on panel data as well.

# Description
Environmental Management and Regulatory Compliance 
The course aims at teaching students the principles of corporate sustainability and environmental management as well as the major environmental laws and good compliance practices in Hong Kong. The course consists of: Part I on Environmental Management covering essential topics including environmental management for sustainability, corporate environmental challenges and governance, ESG reporting, environmental and energy management systems; and Part ll on Regulatory Compliance covering major environmental laws and compliance practices in Hong Kong.

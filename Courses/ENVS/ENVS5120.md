# Description
Occupational Health and Safety 
This course will cover the recognition, evaluation and control of various physical, chemical, biological and ergonomic hazards encountered in the occupational environment. Emphases will focus on the nature and impact of these hazards, assessment methods and corresponding control measures to ensure protection of workers' health and compliance with regulatory requirements.

# Description
Solar Energy Science and Engineering 
Solar energy is the most prominent renewable energy option for our carbon-neutral future. If efficiently and cheaply harnessed, the solar energy available on the surface of the earth is sufficient to meet the energy requirements of the entire planet while creating minimum carbon footprint. Therefore, the efforts in the past decades, developing solar energy science and technology, have been significant. This course will elaborate the key science and engineering topics of solar energies, especially focusing on photovoltaic and photothermal devices.

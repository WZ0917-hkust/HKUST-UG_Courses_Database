# Description
Energy Policy 
This course aims to equip the students with the knowledge and skills to understand, analyse and create innovative energy policies for application in public or business sectors. It will give an energy overview and challenges, a detail review of energy management tools available, and a critical understanding of the role of policy that will integrate all these essential elements to achieve a sustainable development.

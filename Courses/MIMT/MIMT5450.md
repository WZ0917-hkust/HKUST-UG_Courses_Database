# Description
Entrepreneurial Strategy for the Digital and Global Era 
This curated course offers a window to the real-world entrepreneurial journey, using contemporary case studies, class and online discussions, and collaborative projects. These enable students to better grasp fundamental issues in entrepreneurship: addressing root causes of failure, identifying a winning Minimum Viable Product (MVP), attracting investors, and succeeding in platform and data-driven businesses. Students emerge equipped to launch or join new ventures or lead entrepreneurial initiatives within established organizations.

# Description
Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) in China 
This course aims at equipping the next-generation business leaders with knowledge and competence to navigate the nascent, rapidly evolving environmental, social, and governance (ESG) landscape in China, effectively assess and manage the rising ESG risks and opportunities, and seek long-term value creation by taking into account the needs of all their stakeholders and society at large.

# Description
Strategic AI: Transforming Business and Redefining Leadership
This course focuses on the strategic application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in business, emphasizing human-centred approaches and leadership in the AI era. Designed to equip MBA students with some of the latest AI tools and applications, this course covers key application areas such as innovation, operational efficiency, customer experience enhancement, and ethical considerations. Through case studies, practical exercises, discussions, and guest speaker interviews, students will learn to leverage AI to drive business success. Each week features a guest speaker who shares real-time situations and challenges from their organizations, providing practical perspectives on the material. This course offers a comprehensive overview of cutting-edge AI practices, preparing students to apply these insights in their careers. Graded P of F.

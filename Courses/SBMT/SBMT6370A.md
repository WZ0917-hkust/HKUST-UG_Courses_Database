# Description
Global Network Week 
The course aims to integrate global horizons and foster cross-cultural, economic, and social understanding by immersing MBA/MSc students in another culture. Students will have the opportunity to study a short-term program at one of the top business schools of the Global Network for Advanced Management (GNAM) and learn from leading professionals in selected locations globally. Some of the GNAM member schools include INSEAD, HEC Paris, UC Berkeley, LSE, IE Business School, etc. Typically the program will include lectures on-campus and off-campus, corporate visits, and meeting with local business leaders, etc. This course may be repeated for credits if students participate more than once in Global Network Week. Graded P or F.

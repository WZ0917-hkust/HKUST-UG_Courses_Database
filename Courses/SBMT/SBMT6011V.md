# Description
Marketing Analytics: Driving Business Decisions
This course will provide MBA students with an overview of some of the latest trends and applied tools in marketing analytics essential for their first post-MBA positions. Designed to develop fluency in current marketing analytics practices, the course covers key application areas such as digital marketing, consumer behavior analysis, predictive analytics, and ethical data use. Students will learn to evaluate marketing performance, understand market trends, and communicate insights effectively by integrating theoretical insights, practical applications, and case studies. Each week features a guest speaker who shares real-time situations and challenges from their organizations, providing practical perspectives on the material. Graded P of F.

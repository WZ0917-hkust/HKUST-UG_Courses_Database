# Description
Business Practice for Business Analytics 
The course consists of twofold: 1) Refreshment of basic accounting and finance, statistics, and programming skills for students to apply to the MSc in Business Analytics program; and 2) Develop and broaden students' creative mindset, business sense, and communication through training in data management, design thinking, and business communication. In-class exercises and assignments may be required depending on individual workshop design. An exemption for partial workshop training may be granted if students fulfill the exemption requirement. Graded PP, P or F.

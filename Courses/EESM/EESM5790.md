# Description
Business for Electronic Engineers 
This course intends to provide engineering students with an introduction to the knowledge required in the conduction of business. Topics covered include: the organization of technology enterprises, fundamentals of financial and managerial accounting, engineering economics, operations and supply chain, quality processes, mindset of the marketer and the five steps of marketing, managerial finance, understanding of an enterprise from its financial statements, technology and entrepreneurship, and case studies.

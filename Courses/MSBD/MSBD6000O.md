# Description
FinTech 
This FinTech course dives into the evolving landscape of financial technology. Students will gain a thorough understanding of the diverse participants within the financial industry, from traditional banks to disruptive start-ups. The curriculum emphasizes practical application, equipping learners with the skills to harness cutting-edge technology in various financial practice areas. Through a blend of case studies, hands-on projects, and interactive discussions, students will explore the integration of technology in financial services, ensuring they are well-prepared to drive innovation in this dynamic sector.

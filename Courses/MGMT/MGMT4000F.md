# Description
Power, Influence, and Persuasion 
This is an advanced experiential course allowing students to explore and practice the strategies of power and influence experienced in the business world. The course connects scientific theory with relevant practice and focuses on experimenting with specific techniques to achieve desired outcomes in investing, management, and entrepreneurship contexts.

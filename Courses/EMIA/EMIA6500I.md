# Description
Art Tech Curatorship: Concepts and Practices 
This course provides students with foundational knowledge of art tech curatorship, focusing on the integration of technology in contemporary art practices. By the end of the course, students will be able to analyze, design, and implement art exhibitions that effectively incorporate digital technology and interactive elements. They will engage in hands-on prototyping sessions to explore various technologies such as augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and generative AI. Additionally, students will participate in workshops that emphasize collaborative problem-solving and creative brainstorming, allowing them to develop practical skills in installation and project management for cultural spaces like the Fringe Club and Tai Kwun in Hong Kong. Industry professionals will share their insights through guest lectures and interactive workshops, fostering a participatory learning environment.

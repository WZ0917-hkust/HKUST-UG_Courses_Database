# Description
Cross-disciplinary Seminar: Digital Media and Creative Arts 
A compulsory course for students enrolling in the extended major programs throughout their course of study. It is a seminar series presented by faculty members and guest speakers on interdisciplinary topics in extended major area and its applications related to the major. For students with extended major in their second year of study only. Graded P or F.

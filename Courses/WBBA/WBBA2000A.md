# Description
WBB Finance Accelerator
This course aims to provide WBB students with basic financial skills required in preparing for case competitions and future courses in finance. The Finance Accelerator will explore basic financial statement analysis, using major financial ratios. It will cover time value of money, discounted cash flow calculations, NPV and IRR, and cash flow forecasting. For WBB students and students with instructors' approval only. Graded P or F.

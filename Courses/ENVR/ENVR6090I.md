# Description
Global and National Perspectives and Approaches on Green Finance Governance 
This graduate-level course gives students a broad view of the rise of sustainable development and what financing sustainability means. Although the concepts and financing tools were first articulated and shaped in developed economies, the current challenge is not only for developed economies to achieve sustainability but also for emerging economies (such as much of Asia) to develop sustainably, taking climate change, biodiversity, and socially positive goals into account. The course will explore various approaches from the Global North and Global South to achieving and financing sustainability, incorporating real-world case studies to illustrate key concepts. Expert guest speakers will provide hands-on experience and share insights into the challenges and opportunities in steering capital towards environmentally and socially responsible outcomes.

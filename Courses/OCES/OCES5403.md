# Description
Deep Sea Resources 
This course is designed for first-year postgraduate students. The objective of this course is to introduce the geology of the deep sea, the mining of deep sea resources, the diversity of organisms in the deep sea, the sampling of deep sea organisms, and how the deep sea resources should be regulated and protected.

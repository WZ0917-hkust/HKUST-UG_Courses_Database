# Description
Research Project in Global Marine Resources Management I 
This course enables students to conduct an independent project in selected areas of marine resources management under the supervision of one or more faculty members. This course is the first part of a two-term research project. Students shall consult the list of topics provided by the faculty members and choose to perform a research project and formulate an individual study plan. This course is for MSc(GMRM) students only.

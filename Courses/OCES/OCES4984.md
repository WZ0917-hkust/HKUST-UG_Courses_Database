# Description
Ocean Science and Technology Research Project II 
This course is a continuation of OCES 4974 in which students will undertake a research project about ocean science and technology under faculty supervision. Through the reading of scientific literature, field/laboratory studies, meetings with supervisors, and presentations in laboratory meetings, students will learn the latest knowledge in their chosen field of research and the skills in hypothesis synthesis and testing, experimental design, data collection and analysis, and scientific communication. Students will be evaluated for their performance through continuous assessment by the supervisor and a written report to be submitted at the end of the course.

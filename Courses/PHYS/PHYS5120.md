# Description
Computational Energy Materials and Electronic Structure Simulations 
This course will introduce atomistic computational methods to model, understand, and predict the properties and behavior of real materials including solids, liquids, and nanostructures. Their applications to sustainable energy will be discussed. Specific topics include: density-functional theory (DFT), Kohn-Sham equations, local and semi-local density approximations and hybrid functionals, basis sets, pseudopotentials; Hartree-Fock method; ab initio molecular dynamics with interatomic interactions derived on the ï¬y from DFT, Car-Parrinello molecular dynamics; Monte-Carlo sampling; computational spectroscopy from ï¬rst principles, IR and Raman.

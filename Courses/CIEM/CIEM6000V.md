# Description
Hydrosystems Engineering and Management 
This course will cover the fundamental and technical aspects of various hydrosystems and their management. A major focus is to bring together the use of hydrology, hydraulics, and water resources for the analysis, design, operation, and management of water-related projects. Topics include global water cycle, surface water system, groundwater system, urban water system design, stormwater management, and engineering economic analysis.

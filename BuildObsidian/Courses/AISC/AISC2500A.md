# Description
Learning How to Learn in the Age of AI 
In an era defined by rapid technological advancement, "Learning to Learn in the Age of AI" equips students with essential skills and strategies to navigate the complexities of modern education. This course explores foundational concepts of learning while integrating the transformative impact of artificial intelligence on educational practices. Students will engage with diverse learning theories, cognitive processes, and metacognitive strategies, enabling them to understand how they learn best. The curriculum emphasizes practical applications of effective study techniques, such as active learning, spaced repetition, and personalized learning experiences using AI tools. Throughout the course, students will critically examine the ethical considerations and future implications of AI in education, fostering a holistic view of learning in a tech-driven environment. Through interactive lectures, collaborative activities, and reflective practices, participants will develop personalized learning plans that enhance their academic performance and prepare them for lifelong learning in a rapidly evolving world. By the end of the course, students will not only gain a deeper understanding of their own learning processes but also acquire the tools to leverage technology effectively, ensuring they remain adaptable and successful learners in the age of AI.

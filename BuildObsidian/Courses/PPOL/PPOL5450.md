# Description
Comparative Social Welfare Policy 
This course teaches students basic theories and practices of social welfare policies through an international comparative perspective. Key policy areas include poverty alleviation, health care, old-age protection, elderly care, public housing, and labor protection. A variety of pedagogies will be used: seminar, movie-screening, and guest lecture.

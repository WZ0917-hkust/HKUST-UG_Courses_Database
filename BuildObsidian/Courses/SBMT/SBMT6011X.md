# Description
Crisis Leadership
The primary objective of this course is to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of crisis leadership and management. Students will explore the intricacies of crisis situations, differentiating between crises, issues, and risks. Through engaging discussions and practical examples, they will grasp the fundamental principles of effective crisis mitigation strategies. This foundational knowledge will empower them to navigate the new landscape of crises, including challenges posed by emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence and the prevalence of disinformation today. 

In addition to theoretical understanding, students will acquire essential skills in reputation management and communication strategies. By diving into case studies and a simulation, they will learn the fundamentals of effective messaging, manage stakeholder perceptions, and employ strategic tools for crisis action plans. The course places significant emphasis on the importance of trust and its restoration, allowing students to analyze trust dynamics within organizations. Ultimately, through a blend of practical application and reflective learning, students will be prepared to lead with confidence and resilience in times of crisis. Graded P or F.

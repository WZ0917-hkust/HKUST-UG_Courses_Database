# Description
Social Enterprise Internship Program
The course is intended to give business undergraduate students an opportunity to gain exposure in the area of social entrepreneurship and apply their knowledge to solve real-world challenges facing these organizations. It is conducted in the form of an internship program at a designated social enterprise under the supervision of the School of Business and Management. Approval from the course coordinator is needed prior to enrolling in the course. Graded P or F. Students may repeat the course for credits if different topics are taken.

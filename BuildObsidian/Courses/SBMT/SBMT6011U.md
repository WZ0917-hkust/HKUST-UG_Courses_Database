# Description
Effective People Decisions: Unlocking the Power of Talent
This course is designed to empower MBA students with the skills and insights needed to make impactful people decisions and maximize their full career potential. By delving into the art and science of making smart people decisions, students will learn how to recruit the right talents for the right roles in order to build high-performing teams, nurture individual potential, and drive organizational success. Through interactive discussions, real-world case studies, and fun exercises, students will gain the confidence and expertise to define talent gaps, needs, screen, attract, retain and develop the right people on their teams.

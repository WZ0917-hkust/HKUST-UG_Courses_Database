# Description
Managing the Workforce of the Future: Emerging Generations and Work Paradigms
This course is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills required to manage a diverse, multi-generational workforce effectively. The course focuses on understanding the unique characteristics and work preferences of different generational cohorts, analyzing the impact of cultural changes and technological advancements on workplace dynamics, and developing strategies for leveraging a multigenerational and multicultural workforce. By exploring new career models, remote and hybrid work environments, and the implications of ageism, students will gain practical strategies for fostering a harmonious and productive work environment. The course consists of four detailed classes that incorporate engaging case discussions, in-class simulations, and a 'Dream Team' project, culminating in a presentation exercise. These elements provide a safe environment for students to test and refine their leadership skills in managing a multi-generational and multicultural workforce. Through this course, students will gain a new perspective on perennial-influenced work culture and insights into the future of corporate structures, including the impact of Decentralized Autonomous Organizations ( DAOs). By the end of the course, students will understand their position as transformative leaders, recognize the role of ageism, and see how generational differences influence decisions and behaviors in various career opportunities, especially as the business world evolves with forces such as GenAI and Web3.

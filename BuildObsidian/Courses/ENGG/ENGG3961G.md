# Description
Robotics Special Project: MATE ROV Competition 2025 
The Marine Advanced Technology Education (MATE) Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) Competition is an international competition for young engineers. Student teams will design and manufacture the underwater robot for tackling different missions and challenges which are provided by the organizer. Normally, a project report is required. Enrollment in the project course may require instructor's approval. May be graded PP.

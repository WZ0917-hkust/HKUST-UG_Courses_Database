# Description
Community Services Project: STEAM Project for the Community 
The aim of this course is to provide more chances for UG student self-development, let them not only apply what have learnt from the course, but also acquire different kinds of hand-on experiences by delivering the designed STEAM program to the community. In so doing, the 1st semester will learn the 21st century skill requirement of Education, while in 2nd Semester, they will have chance to deliver their design program and serve the community. Approval from the course coordinator is needed prior to enrolling in the course. Graded PP, P or F. Students may repeat the course for credits if different topics are taken.

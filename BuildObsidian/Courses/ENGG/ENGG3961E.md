# Description
Robotics Special Project: RoboMasters 2025 Robot Contest 
RoboMasters Robot Contest is a robot competition platform created for young engineers. Participating teams will get out of the classroom and independently develop and manufacture robots for the shooting confrontation. The contest will provide them with an opportunity to obtain precious practical skills and use strategic thinking, combine theories with practice and create advanced smart robots through fierce competition. Each year, new game field, rules of competition (https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/robo/rm) and a new technical challenge are introduced to the competition. Enrollment in the project course may require instructor's approval. May be graded PP.

# Description
Developmental Psychology 
Developmental psychology is the scientific study of how we grow and change over the lifespan. This course will guide students to investigate physical, cognitive, and psychosocial development from infancy to late adulthood from a psychological perspective. Students should complete the course with a deeper understanding of key theories, research methods, and research findings on human development.

# Description
Research Methods in the Social Sciences 
The course is designed to assist students in developing a basic understanding of qualitative and quantitative data analysis and interpretation in the social sciences. Background knowledge of statistics is not required for taking this course. Not to be taken by students admitted to BSc Quantitative Social Analysis from 2022-23 and onwards.

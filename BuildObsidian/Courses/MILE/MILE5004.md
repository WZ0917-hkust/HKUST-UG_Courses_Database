# Description
Understanding Research in Language Education 
The course will provide students with an introduction to the principles of research in language education and relevant research methods. The course will begin with an examination of key dimensions of research, then move on to look at research methodologies from a theoretical and practical perspective. For assessment, students will critically review published research in language education, and will respond to questions requiring them to demonstrate their understanding of key concepts in language education research and their ability to evaluate methods commonly used in the field. For MA(ILE) students or students with program director or course instructor's approval only.

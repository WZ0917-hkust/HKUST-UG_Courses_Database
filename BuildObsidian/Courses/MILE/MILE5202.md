# Description
Teaching Second Language Vocabulary 
This course examines the factors involved in developing second language (L2) learners' acquisition of English vocabulary. It covers three main areas: (a) words and the description of the vocabulary content of an English curriculum, (b) the representation of L2 vocabulary in the mental lexicon and related acquisitor issues, and (c) approaches to teaching L2 vocabulary. For MA(ILE) students or students with program director or course instructor's approval only.

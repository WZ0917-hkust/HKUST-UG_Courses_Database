# Description
Navigating Capital Markets: A Practitioners Guide to Capital Flows 
This course offers and in-depth exploration into the realm of global capital markets in practice. Together we will delve into the inner workings of financial markets, including primary and secondary markets, trading floors and market dynamics. Students will apply analytical skills to learn to identify opportunities across capital markets including synthesizing information, identifying capital uses and interpreting market sentiment and behaviour. This course is designed to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and practical application. The focus is on equipping students with the insights needed to excel in the complex world of capital markets providing a foundation for exploring more advanced subjects.

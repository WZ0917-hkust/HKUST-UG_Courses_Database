# Description
AI and the Future of Finance
This MBA course delves into the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on the finance industry. Focusing on practical applications, strategic implementation, and future trends, the course explores how AI is reshaping various sectors within finance, including banking, capital markets, investment management, payments, insurance, and risk management.

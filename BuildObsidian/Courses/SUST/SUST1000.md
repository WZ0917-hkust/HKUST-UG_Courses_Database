# Description
Sustainability Fundamentals 
This course provides students with an interdisciplinary perspective in comprehending the challenging issues on human sustainability on the limited planet earth. It also provides an overarching framework covering economic development, individual lifestyle and policies to deal with global, regional and local emerging issues to provide students with a good foundation for their sustainability related majors and minors.

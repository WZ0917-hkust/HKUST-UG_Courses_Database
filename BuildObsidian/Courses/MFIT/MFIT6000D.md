# Description
Exploring Virtual Assets: Opportunities and Challenges 
This course explores the evolving landscape of Web 3.0 and virtual assets and focus on Hong Kongâs developing role as a virtual asset hub. Students will explore different digital asset classes, from cryptocurrencies, security tokens, VA ETF, CBDC, to stablecoins. After the course, students will understand their business models, underlying technological frameworks and regulatory requirements. Emphasis of the course are on the industry practices and implementations. The course covers key developments In virtual asset trading platforms, tokenization of traditional assets, virtual asset ETFs, OTC trading and stablecoin issuance. Special attention is given to regulatory framework in Hong Kong, including the SFC VASP licensing regime, VA regulations, and policy initiatives including OTC and stablecoin issuance. Students will learn about practical applications as well as the regulatory challenges.

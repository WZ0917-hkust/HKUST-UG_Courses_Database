# Description
Financial Economics: Corporate Finance 
This course applies Industrial Organization to examine crucial corporate finance policies such as investment policy, financing policy, and payout policy, and to demonstrate their importance in creating or destroying firm value. Students learn to value any asset using simple, universal principles, and use Microeconomic Theories to analyze corporate governance issues such as agency conflicts and managerial incentives. For MSc(ECON) students only.

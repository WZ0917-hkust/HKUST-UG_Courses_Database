# Description
Bioengineering Colloquia 
Bioengineering Colloquia is a seminar course that brings prominent researchers in the field of bioengineering to share their research with BIEN colleagues and students. The course aims to provide a platform for discussions and exchange of ideas on the late advancements in the field of bioengineering. Students will have the opportunity to engage with the speakers and ask questions, fostering a deeper understanding of the research presented. The course will cover a broad range of topics, including chemical engineering, biotechnology, material sciences, synthetic biology and more. Students are expected to attend regularly. Grade P or F.

# Description
Bioengineering and Synthetic Biology Colloquia
The Bioengineering and Synthetic Biology Colloquia showcases groundbreaking research in the field of bioengineering and synthetic biology. This program invites leading researchers to interact with BIEN faculty, staff, and students, sharing their latest findings and insights. The primary goal is to establish a vibrant forum where participants can delve into the most recent advances in the field, fostering collaboration. The curriculum spans a comprehensive range of topics, including biological engineering, biotechnology, synthetic biology, and so on. Grade P or F.

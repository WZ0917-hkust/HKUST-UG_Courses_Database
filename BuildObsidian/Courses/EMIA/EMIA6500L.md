# Description
Artificial Intelligence for Financial Economics 
This course is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of artificial intelligence (AI), with a particular emphasis on its applications in economics and finance. It aims to demystify the complexities of AI through a case-study approach focused on its deployment in various economic and financial scenarios. By exploring a range of business case studies, the course will illustrate how AI can address complex challenges across multiple sectors. Students from diverse disciplines will gain critical knowledge and hands-on experience to leverage AI strategies for business innovation. This course is specifically tailored for individuals in extended major programs who wish to develop a deep, practical mastery of AI for business applications.

Capstone Project for Data Analytics in Science 
Students are expected to complete a project in an area of their study tracks under the guidance of a faculty member. It provides an opportunity for students to integrate those mathematical and data analytics techniques they learned to analyze a data intensive problem in science. For DASC students in their fourth year of study only.

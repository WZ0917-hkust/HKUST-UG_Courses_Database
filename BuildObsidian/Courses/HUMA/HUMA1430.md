# Description
History of P. R. China's Diplomacy, 1949-1989 
This course will introduce to students the diplomatic history of the People's Republic of China (PRC) from 1949 to 1989, focusing on Sino-USSR and Sino-U.S. relations in the context of the Cold War. Key events and leading figures will be covered, newly declassified archival materials will be presented, and critical examination and rethinking of PRC history will be introduced for discussion.

# Description
History of Chinese Philosophy 
This course aims at a historical survey of the developments of Chinese philosophy. Through an introduction to the great Chinese thinkers (like Confucius and Mo Zi), the main schools of thought to which they belong (like Confucianism and Mohism), their central theories (like humanity and universal love), and some other important philosophical concepts and issues, students will be guided to explore the evolution of these philosophies against the background of Chinese history and civilization.

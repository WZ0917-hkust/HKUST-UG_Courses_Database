# Description
Global History: New Anthropological Perspectives 
This course takes on the broad sweep of human history from an anthropological perspective, highlighting the great social and cultural diversity of human experience. Popular accounts of global history depict human society evolving stepwise in a unilinear fashion: egalitarian hunter-gatherers gave way to hierarchical agricultural societies before the rise of cities and, later, the establishment of states. New archaeological and anthropological discoveries are now challenging such picture: the trajectory of human history, it turns out, has been much more diverse and unpredictable than previously imagined. They cast light on the past while also providing insights into the possibilities of the future.

# Description
Introduction to Acting and Public Speaking 
Acting, improvisation, and public speaking are artforms at the core of many arenas of communication and human striving. Whether it is the performance that moves the hearts and minds of the audience, on stage or on screen, or the theatre of a boardroom meeting, a wedding toast, or a university lecture, the foundational skills of acting and public speaking come into play. In this course students will receive an introductory exposure to the history and techniques of modern acting and public speaking, including elements of improve, and learn how they can be used to enhance other activities as well as promote a self-awareness and mindfulness.

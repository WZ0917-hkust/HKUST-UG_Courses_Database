# Description
Laboratory for General Biology I
This is a practical course accompanied by the lecture course LIFS 1901. It provides students with some basic concepts and some hands-on experience in biological investigation within the areas covered by LIFS 1901. The emphasis is on the understanding and application of the scientific principles underlying the experimental designs and procedures. Graded P or F. For students in their first, second year of study; or third or fourth year students with instructor's approval.

# Description
Supply Chain Management 
This course will provide students with fundamental knowledge in operations management and supply chain management, focusing on topics related to the management of business process that produce and deliver goods or services in a global business environment. Topics include process analysis and improvement, inventory and revenue management, supply chain strategies, supply chain integration and coordination, and other important issues.

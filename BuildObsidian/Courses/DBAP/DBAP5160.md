# Description
Frontiers in Management Research
This introductory course exposes students to the latest research and trends in management research (e.g., organizational behavior, human resources management, strategic management, international business, entrepreneurship, and business ethics). Students will read and critique latest research papers from selected topics and participate in class discussion. Students are expected to understand management issues of current and future concerns, identify an interesting and practically important frontier research question in a management topic of choice, and write an executable research proposal to address the question.

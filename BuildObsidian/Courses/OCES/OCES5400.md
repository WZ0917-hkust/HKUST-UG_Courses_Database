# Description
Marine Genomics 
This is an advanced marine genomics course for first-year postgraduate students. It covers the fundamental knowledge of marine genomics and the applications of cutting-edge genomics tools in a marine setting. This course will provide the students with the essential skills for conducting research in the emerging field of marine genomics.

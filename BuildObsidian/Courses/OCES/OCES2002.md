# Description
Marine Chemistry 
This course is an introduction to chemical oceanography. It describes the chemistry of the ocean based on distribution and dynamics of elements, isotopes, atoms and molecules. This ranges from fundamental physical, thermodynamic and kinetic chemistry to two-way interactions of ocean chemistry with biological, geological and physical processes. It encompasses both inorganic and organic chemistry, and includes studies of ocean-atmosphere dynamics and marine sediments.

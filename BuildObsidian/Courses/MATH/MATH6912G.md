# Description
Reading Course: Computer Methods for DAEs 
This course provides an in-depth exploration of differential-algebraic equations (DAEs), focusing on both theoretical foundations and practical applications. Students will engage with advanced topics, including existence and uniqueness theorems, stability analysis, and numerical methods for solving ODEs and DAEs. Students should seek approval from the course instructor to take this reading course.

# Description
Reading Course: Conformally Invariant Processes in the Plane 
Conformally invariant processes in the plane have been instrumental in progress on (critical) planar statistical mechanics models in the last three decades. As one particular case in point, the work of Lawler, Schramm, and Werner on Stochastic Loewner Evolutions (SLE) together with Smirnovâs proof of conformal invariance for the continuum limit of site percolation on the triangular lattice yields a rigorous verification of certain critical exponents for two-dimensional percolation. A working knowledge of measure-theoretic probability theory (at the level of MATH 5411 Advanced Probability Theory I) is useful to follow this course.  A good background in Complex Analysis including the residue theorem (at the level of MATH 4023 Complex Analysis) or the willingness to self-study it is also required. Students should seek approval from the course instructor to take this reading course.

# Description
Reading Course: Low-Rank Tensor Recovery and Applications
We will study both theory and applications of low-rank tensor recovery. The low-rank property of the tensors under our consideration is the low-tensor-train rank, and the applications include quantum state tomography and image reconstruction. Students should seek approval from the course instructor to take this reading course.

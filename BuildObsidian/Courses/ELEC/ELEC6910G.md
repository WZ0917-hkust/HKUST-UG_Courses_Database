# Description
Introduction to Biorobot: from Nature to Engineering 
Biorobotics is an interdisciplinary field that encompasses biomedical engineering, cybernetics, robotics, and more. This course aims to provide an introduction to the fundamental concepts and knowledge of biorobots. It will cover the description and analysis of biorobots with various types of nature-inspired locomotion and behavior. Additionally, it will explore different architectures and scales of biorobots; and discuss advanced techniques and interdisciplinary knowledge required for their development.

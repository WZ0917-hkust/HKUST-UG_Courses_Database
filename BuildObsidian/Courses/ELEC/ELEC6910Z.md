# Description
Modern Solid State Devices 
This course aims to provide students with the up-to-date research and development progress of solid state devices and technologies in the 21st century, with a focus on emerging materials and physical phenomena of modern electronics and optoelectronics. Emphasis will be placed on the interdisciplinary nature of present solid-state research, bridging the gap between fundamental physics, materials science, and practical device engineering.

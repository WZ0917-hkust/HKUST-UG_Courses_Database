# Description
Modern Chinese History 
This course aims at exploring the political and social history of late Imperial and modern China from the 1800s to early 1940s. A selection of important political events and social phenomena are examined to broaden our knowledge of the late-Qing and Republican politics, society and economy as well as revealing the complexity of historical reality in this period.

# Description
Risk Management and Decision-Making in Intelligent Building 
This course aims to provide an understanding of the definition of risk, perception of risk, social acceptability of risk, risk assessment and management techniques, principle and process of risk management, contingency plans and disaster recovery, principle and process of risk-informed decision, multi-attribute utility theory, analytical hierarchical process and cost-risk benefit analysis in intelligent building management.

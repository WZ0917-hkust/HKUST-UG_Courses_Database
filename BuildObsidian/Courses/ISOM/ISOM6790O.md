# Description
Managing Business to Create Value through Sustainability 
This course explores the practical applications of sustainability principles in the context of business operations, strategy, and innovation. Students will examine how organizations can integrate sustainability practices to create long-term value, mitigate risks, enhance competitive advantage, and contribute to addressing the grand challenge of the climate crisis. The course will cover key sustainability frameworks, tools, and best practices that can be applied across various business functions.

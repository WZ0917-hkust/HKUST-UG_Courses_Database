# Description
Visualizing Data for Business Decisions
A clear and concise communication of results is essential to a successful business analytics endeavor. In the context of business communication, data visualization could help deliver analytics results or tell stories by curating data into a form easy to understand and navigate, with the valuable information and insights explicitly highlighted. Furthermore, interactive visualization could also be combined with automated analysis to provide business decision makers an effective way to explore a large data set, make interpretation, drill down to uncover hidden patterns and deeper insights, so as to leverage their domain knowledge to guide the data analytics process. Graded P or F.

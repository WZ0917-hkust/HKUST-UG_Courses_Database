# Description
Generative AI and Large Language Models 
The course covers the history and technology behind Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs), emphasizing their applications, industry impact, and limitations. Students explore these transformative technologies hands-on, gaining essential knowledge for understanding their principles and practical uses in business. This prepares them to innovate and adapt within the evolving landscape of LLM technology.

# Description
Digital Strategy and Transformation 
Digital strategy and transformation are critical in almost every industry. This course covers two main themes to prepare students to contribute to digital initiatives in their future workplace. First, we will learn frameworks and concepts to help organizations develop their digital strategy. Second, we will examine how public and private organizations can and should implement digital transformation projects to improve stakeholder value. This course is designed for Year 3 and Year 4 students who are interested in IT and/or consulting careers.

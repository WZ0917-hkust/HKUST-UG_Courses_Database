# Description
Social Media: Collective Intelligence & Creativity 
Web technology is now connecting a diversity of people and ideas and encouraging cooperation and collaboration. However, the nascent proliferation of fake news is beginning to have a corrosive effect on the open and peer-to-peer collaborations that are the underpinning of Web 2.0. The aim of the course is to make students aware of the tug of war between beneficial and harmful effects of this social media phenomenon.
